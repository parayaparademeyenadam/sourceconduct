Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This notice explains how SourceConduct handles information when you visit sourceconduct.com. It focuses on website analytics. If you contact us by email, we will also process the information you choose to include in your message so that we can respond.

Who is responsible?

SourceConduct is responsible for the processing described in this notice. For privacy questions or to exercise a data protection right, contact licensing@sourceconduct.com.

Optional website analytics

If you consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this website. This can include pages visited, interactions, approximate geographic location inferred from technical information, device and browser information, referral sources, and engagement time. We use these insights to improve the website and understand which content is useful.

Google Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Accept analytics” in the consent banner. If you reject optional analytics, it remains disabled. You can change your choice at any time using the “Privacy settings” control shown on the website.

Our website stores your consent choice in your browser so it can remember that choice. This preference storage is used to operate the consent mechanism; it is not used to track your browsing activity.

Legal basis

We rely on your consent for optional analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time by opening Privacy settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew consent.

Google and international processing

Google provides Google Analytics. When analytics is enabled, information may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's terms and privacy documentation. Depending on how Google services are provided, data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate safeguards must apply. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Analytics safeguards data.

Retention

Analytics retention is subject to the settings configured in the Google Analytics property and Google's applicable policies. The consent preference is reviewed after six months, at which point the website asks you to choose again.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of processing, or to object to certain processing. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Contact licensing@sourceconduct.com to make a request.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority: dataprotectionauthority.be.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website or processing changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Important: This notice describes the analytics setup currently intended for this website. It should be reviewed against the legal identity and contact details of the operating business, the actual Google Analytics configuration, and any other services or data processing introduced later.
