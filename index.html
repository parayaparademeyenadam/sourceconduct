We are open-source licensing specialists, combining technical expertise with deep knowledge of open-source licenses across four key areas.

1. You use open-source software in your applications. What are your rights, risks and obligations?

We help you assess how open-source components are used in your applications, interpret the relevant license terms, and understand the obligations, risks, and options available to you. This gives you a clearer basis for making informed technical and business decisions while managing compliance proactively.

Let's talk ↗

2. You're advising on an open-source licensing matter. Need help understanding the technology?

We work alongside legal counsel to examine software architecture, dependencies, implementation, deployment and technical evidence. We help distinguish assumptions from facts, clarify how the software actually behaves, and identify the technical details that matter to the licensing analysis and any next steps.

Let's talk ↗

3. You own an open-source project. What can you do when someone may be violating its license?

We help you address open-source license violations and develop effective enforcement strategies. Beyond individual cases, we can design and build an open-source licensing program tailored to your organization, leveraging our experience building such programs at other organizations. We help you turn non-compliant users into paying customers and transform open-source licensing into a sustainable, substantial revenue stream.

Let's talk ↗

4. You have valuable software. Is there a better way to turn adoption into revenue?

We help you evaluate your software, current adoption and licensing approach to identify where commercial value may be going unrealized. Together, we can explore licensing models and practical strategies that support compliance, convert adoption into paying customers, and build a more sustainable source of revenue.

Who we are

Technical enough to understand the code. Experienced enough to know when the legal argument doesn't hold up.

SourceConduct was founded by a computer engineer with a PhD who started his career as a software developer, working hands-on with the technologies and open source components that power modern software.

That perspective later moved to the other side of the table: open source licensing and enforcement. He built and developed a license compliance program for a leading global open source software company, turning the company's open source adoption and licensing issues into a structured, scalable business function and a significant source of revenue.

Over the years, he has worked with senior lawyers and legal teams across jurisdictions on complex open source licensing matters, helping companies understand their exposure, addressing compliance issues and bringing licensing disputes to resolution.

This combination of engineering experience, licensing expertise and commercial perspective is what SourceConduct brings to the table. We understand the software, the license and the business context around it.

We don't start with a legal theory. We start by understanding what the software actually does, how it is being used, and what the license means in that real-world context.
