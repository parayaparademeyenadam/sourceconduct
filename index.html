Using open source

“We use open source in our applications. What are our rights and obligations?”

Enforcing your rights

“We own an open source project. What happens when someone isn't respecting the license?”

You maintain or own open source software and believe a user or company may not be complying with its license. We provide guidance on identifying the issue, understanding your enforcement rights under GPL, AGPL and other licenses, and deciding how to engage.

Who we are

Technical enough to understand the code. Experienced enough to know when the legal argument doesn't hold up.

SourceConduct was founded by a computer engineer with a PhD who started his career as a software developer, working hands-on with the technologies and open source components that power modern software.

That perspective later moved to the other side of the table: open source licensing and enforcement. He built and developed a license compliance program for a leading global open source software company, turning the company's open source adoption and licensing issues into a structured, scalable business function and a significant source of revenue.

Over the years, he has worked with senior lawyers and legal teams across jurisdictions on complex open source licensing matters, helping companies understand their exposure, addressing compliance issues and bringing licensing disputes to resolution.

This combination of engineering experience, licensing expertise and commercial perspective is what SourceConduct brings to the table. We understand the software, the license and the business context around it.

We don't start with a legal theory. We start by understanding what the software actually does, how it is being used, and what the license means in that real-world context.
