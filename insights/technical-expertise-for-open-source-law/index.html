Technical insight for legal counsel

Open source licensing disputes need technical insight.

The legal analysis is only as strong as the understanding of the software behind it. SourceConduct helps law firms connect open source license terms with technical reality.

When a company faces an open source licensing dispute, it turns to legal counsel for advice. But reaching a sound legal position can depend on understanding how the software actually works.

What does the license mean in the context of the client's software architecture, and what is the technical evidence telling us?

A library listed as a dependency is not necessarily used in the same way as a library integrated into a distributed application. A standalone product raises different questions from a component embedded in a larger system. How components interact, what is distributed, and how software is deployed can all affect the technical analysis.

These questions require more than reading the license text. They require an understanding of software engineering alongside practical knowledge of open source licensing.

A technical partner for legal counsel

SourceConduct works alongside law firms handling open source licensing questions and disputes. We help legal teams investigate the technical facts, understand software architecture and dependencies, assess technical claims, and connect those findings to the relevant license terms.

Our role is to give counsel a clearer technical foundation for its legal analysis, identify questions that need further investigation, and help distinguish assumptions from what the available evidence actually supports.

We bring together software engineering experience and practical open source licensing expertise. We understand that the technical analysis must serve the legal strategy, not operate in isolation from it.
