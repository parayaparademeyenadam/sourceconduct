Technical insight for legal counsel

Open source licensing disputes need technical insight.

The legal analysis is only as strong as the understanding of the software behind it. SourceConduct helps law firms connect open source license terms with technical reality.

When a company faces an open source licensing dispute, it will often turn to experienced legal counsel. But even the strongest legal team may face a difficult question: what does the license mean when applied to the way the software actually works?

Open source licenses do not operate in a vacuum. Understanding their implications can require a close look at software architecture, dependencies, how a library is integrated, how components communicate, and how the product is deployed or offered to users. Small technical distinctions can materially affect the licensing analysis.

A library incorporated into an application may raise different questions from a standalone product or a network-hosted service. Whether software has been modified, how separate components interact, and what is actually distributed or made available can all matter. The answers cannot always be found by reading the license text alone.

That is where SourceConduct can help. We bring software engineering and open source licensing experience to help legal teams understand the technical facts behind the license and assess how those facts may affect their advice.

We work alongside legal counsel, helping them examine the software, ask the right technical questions, and connect license terms to real-world implementation and use. Our role is not to replace legal advice, but to provide the technical understanding that can make it more informed, precise, and practical.

If your firm is advising a client on an open source licensing question or dispute, talk to SourceConduct. A technically grounded view can help you evaluate the situation more clearly and give your client advice that reflects both the legal terms and the software itself.
